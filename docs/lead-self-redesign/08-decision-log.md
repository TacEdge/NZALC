## 1. Current design position (as of 1 Oct 2026)

### 1.0 Operational clarification (1 Oct 2026, supersedes earlier DC-1 assumptions) — SETTLED

These are authoritative design constraints, confirmed by the project lead:

| # | Constraint |
|---|---|
| OC-1 | **Timing.** The two Lead Self days occur in the **first week of TAD, before Exercise One** |
| OC-2 | **Delivery.** NZALC instructors do not normally deliver the two days to recruits. NZALC delivers Lead Self as a **Train the Trainer (T3)** package to TAD staff before the course. The recruit-facing two days are then delivered by the recruits' **Section Commanders (Corporals)** |
| OC-3 | **Continuity.** Those Section Commanders remain with their recruits through TAD. They are the logical source of lightweight coaching, observation and developmental support after the two days. Additional formal Lead Self lessons for Section Commanders are not designed unless clearly necessary; reinforcement is integrated into their normal leadership of the section |
| OC-4 | **LDP ownership.** The recruit develops and owns their LDP, with support from their Section Commander. The Section Commander does not own it |
| OC-5 | **Design freedom.** No known restriction on changing the sequence or content within the two-day package. Existing workbook chapter order is **not** a constraint. Doctrine, LOs and confirmed LDS requirements remain constraints |
| OC-6 | **Workbook use.** Recruits are intended to keep and use the workbook throughout TAD. **In practice they typically do not.** This is treated as a major design problem, not a content problem |

**Ownership model (replaces §1.2):**

| Party | Role |
|---|---|
| **Recruit** | Owner of their development |
| **Section Commander** | Coach, observer and feedback source. **Not** a workbook administrator |
| **Workbook** | Development tool and evidence thread |
| **TAD training** | The environment in which behaviour is practised, pressured and reapplied |
| **NZALC** | Designer of the system, and developer of the Section Commanders through T3 |

**DC-1 restated.** The two days are NZALC-*designed* and Section Commander-*delivered*. "Without further NZALC instruction" still holds. "Without an instructor present" no longer does (see DR-5).

**Consequence: two products.** The redesign eventually covers both the recruit experience/workbook **and** the NZALC T3 package for Section Commanders.

### 1.1 Delivery constraint and the design problem — DC-1 (SETTLED; delivery model clarified in §1.0)

> **DC-1.** NZALC has **two days** of direct Lead Self delivery at TAD. The design must establish a development process capable of continuing beyond those two days **without relying on further NZALC instruction**.

The workbook supports those two days. Lead Self development continues through the rest of the recruit's TAD training.

**This reframes the design problem.**

| Not this | This |
|---|---|
| *How do we fit the existing 91-page workbook into two days?* | *What must a recruit learn, experience, practise and leave with after two days so that they can continue developing themselves through the remainder of TAD?* |

The Phase 1 audit assumed the workbook was used across a course of lessons. Its content findings still stand. Its sequencing assumptions are superseded by DC-1.

### 1.2 Ownership model (SUPERSEDED by §1.0)

| Party | Owns / provides |
|---|---|
| **NZALC** | Two days of deliberate Lead Self instruction and development. These must establish enough **understanding, self-awareness, behavioural language, practical tools, feedback capability, reflection capability and initial development priorities** for the recruit to continue developing after NZALC instructors leave |
| **TAD** | The ongoing training environment in which recruits experience, practise, perform under pressure, work with others, receive consequences and feedback, reapply behaviours, and develop through subsequent training and exercises |
| **The workbook** | Continuity between the two-day NZALC intervention and the recruit's subsequent TAD experience. It should progressively become less of a classroom workbook and more of a simple personal development tool |

**Superseded assumption (see §1.0):** TAD instructors are **not** available to deliver additional Lead Self lessons or administer additional NZALC processes. After the two days, the development process must be **recruit-run and peer-supported**, attached to activity TAD already conducts.

### 1.3 Three structures, connected, not combined (SETTLED, unchanged)

The workbook's job is to **connect** these structures appropriately, not merge them. **DC-1 does not create another model.**

| Structure | Sequence | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **NZALC development method** | LEARN → PRACTICE → PRESSURE → APPLY → REINFORCE | How NZALC develops the recruit |
| **Recruit self-development loop** | EXPERIENCE → FEEDBACK → REFLECT → ADAPT → REAPPLY ↺ | How the recruit develops themselves. REAPPLY becomes the next EXPERIENCE, which closes the loop |
| **Pressure thread** | PREPARE → PERFORM → RECOVER | A frame for pressure and performance activity only. **Not a development loop** |

Under DC-1, the NZALC method is concentrated in the two days. The recruit loop is the engine of everything after. The intended outcome is that the recruit leaves TAD with a **repeatable method for developing themselves**.

### 1.4 Learner-facing framing (SETTLED, unchanged)

- **"Your Lead Self Journey"**, supported conceptually by five questions:
  - *Who am I?*
  - *How do I affect others?*
  - *How do I perform?*
  - *What does the evidence tell me?*
  - *What will I change?*
- These are not necessarily chapters.
- "Orientate" is not used as a stage label.

### 1.5 Emerging two-day intent (PROVISIONAL — design intent, not a timetable)

| | Day 1 — Understand Me and My Impact | Day 2 — Perform, Learn and Develop |
|---|---|---|
| **Likely emphasis** | What Lead Self means; Lead Self expectations / Value Adds; self-awareness; Identity vs Reputation; values and observable behaviour; Above / Below the Line; trust and relationships; giving and receiving feedback | Behaviour under pressure; interference / Comfort Zone; situational awareness; regulation; attention; judgement and decision-making; team contribution; reflection; adapting behaviour; deliberate reapplication; how to use the workbook after the NZALC intervention |
| **Must include** | An early, low-stakes feedback experience, so Identity vs Reputation becomes experiential (DR-2) | Handover of the recruit loop to the recruit |
| **Recruit beginning to answer** | *Who am I? How do I affect others?* | *How do I perform? What does the evidence tell me? What will I change?* |

**Desired end state after two days.** The recruit can say:

> "I understand what effective Lead Self behaviour looks like. I have observed some of my own behaviour. I have received evidence from another person. I have reflected on that evidence. I have identified an initial development priority. I know what I will deliberately do differently. I know how to reapply that behaviour during recruit training. I know how to use subsequent experience and feedback to continue developing myself."

This is **not** yet a two-day lesson programme, and content sequencing is not resolved.

### 1.6 Emerging two-part workbook architecture (PROVISIONAL)

| Part | Purpose | Driven by |
|---|---|---|
| **Part 1 — My Lead Self Foundation** | Primarily supports the two NZALC delivery days | NZALC method (LEARN → … → REINFORCE, within two days) |
| **Part 2 — My Lead Self Development** | Supports continued development through the remainder of TAD | Recruit loop: EXPERIENCE → FEEDBACK → REFLECT → ADAPT → REAPPLY |

- Ex One and Ex Two remain the major evidence and review points unless later evidence requires otherwise (DR-3).
- Lighter feedback and reapplication points between them attach to existing TAD activity wherever possible (DR-4, D17).
- No new administrative burden is created merely to service the workbook.

### 1.7 Combat Mindset / Performance Under Pressure (SETTLED, unchanged)

- Unsettled terminology and doctrine are not resolved. Source terminology and distinctions are preserved.
- Only source-supported integration points are built:

| Integration point | Source support |
|---|---|
| Recognise interference | CN: P = P – I; effects of interference (also workbook p.58) |
| Maintain situational awareness | Workbook pp.34–35; CN "retain judgement and situational awareness" |
| Regulate response | CN definition of mental skills; workbook p.59 |
| Refocus attention | CN "regulate attention"; workbook p.59 (grounding, instructional self-talk) |
| Act | Behaviour Statement "Make decisions in the required time" (p.5); CN "make timely decisions under uncertainty" |
| Review performance | SSR (workbook p.63); CN "structured reflection and recovery" |

- No WIN without an authoritative source.
- Terminology must be swappable late.
- Under DC-1, most integration points fall naturally within the Day 2 emphasis. That is an observation, not a sequencing decision.

## 4. What can begin now, and what needs the timetable

### Can begin now (no TAD timetable needed)

Phase 2 conceptual architecture:

- **What the recruit must leave the two days with.** The end state broken into the capabilities in §1.2 (understanding, self-awareness, behavioural language, tools, feedback and reflection capability, initial priority).
- **The conceptual design of the two days.** What must happen in each day to produce that end state. Not a timetable.
- **The DT-1 topic test** applied to every existing subject.
- **The ST-3 experience test** applied to every existing activity.
- **The design of Part 2 as a recruit-run loop**, at the level of what it asks and produces, independent of when it is used.
- **The evidence trail and the Identity vs Reputation thread**, from the Day 1 baseline to march-out.
- **Placement of the pressure/performance integration points** within the two days and in Part 2.

### Needs the TAD timetable (before locking the architecture)

- Post-NZALC development touchpoints.
- Feedback opportunities between the two days, Ex One and Ex Two.
- Reapplication opportunities and their frequency (D17).
- Placement relative to Ex One and Ex Two, including whether the two days fall before or after either exercise.
- The final end-to-end learner journey.

### Also needed before locking the architecture

- **Assessment constraints** (Q4).
- **LDS-standard reflection tools** (Q6).
- **Who facilitates the post-exercise feedback rounds** (Q12).
