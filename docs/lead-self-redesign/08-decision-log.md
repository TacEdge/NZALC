## 1. Current design position (as of 30 Sep 2026)

### 1.1 Three structures, connected, not combined

The workbook's job is to **connect** these three structures appropriately, not merge them into one cycle.

| Structure | Sequence | Whose is it | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NZALC development method** | LEARN → PRACTICE → PRESSURE → APPLY → REINFORCE | NZALC / instructors | How NZALC develops the recruit |
| **Recruit self-development loop** | EXPERIENCE → FEEDBACK → REFLECT → ADAPT → REAPPLY ↺ | The recruit | How the recruit develops themselves. REAPPLY becomes the next EXPERIENCE, which closes the loop |
| **Pressure thread** | PREPARE → PERFORM → RECOVER | Used within pressure/performance activity | A frame for pressure and performance activity only. **Not a third development loop** |

The intended outcome: by march-out the recruit has not only covered Lead Self content but has **practised a repeatable method for developing themselves** that goes with them after TAD.

### 1.2 Learner-facing framing

- The workbook opens as **"Your Lead Self Journey"**.
- It is supported conceptually by five questions:
  1. *Who am I?*
  2. *How do I affect others?*
  3. *How do I perform?*
  4. *What does the evidence tell me?*
  5. *What will I change?*
- **These are not five mandatory chapters.** Their structural use is a Phase 2 question.
- **"Orientate" is not used** as a workbook stage name, to avoid collision with the LDS *Orientate – Build – Advance* phases (workbook p.3).

### 1.3 Combat Mindset / Performance Under Pressure

- **Unsettled terminology and doctrine are not resolved in this redesign.**
- The terminology and distinctions in the supplied sources (Draft Capability Note; Combat Mindset Framework Proposal) are preserved as they stand, including the difference between Performance Under Pressure and combat-specific expression.
- Only **source-supported integration points** are built:

| Integration point | Source support |
|---|---|
| Recognise interference | CN: Performance = Potential – Interference; effects of interference (also workbook p.58) |
| Maintain situational awareness | Workbook pp.34–35; CN "retain judgement and situational awareness" |
| Regulate response | CN definition of mental skills ("regulate attention, thoughts, emotions and behaviour"); workbook p.59 |
| Refocus attention | CN "regulate attention"; workbook p.59 (grounding, instructional self-talk) |
| Act | Behaviour Statement "Make decisions in the required time" (p.5); CN "make timely decisions under uncertainty" |
| Review performance | Systematic Self Reflection (workbook p.63); CN "structured reflection and recovery" |

- **WIN / What's Important Now is not introduced** unless an authoritative source is supplied. "Refocus attention" remains a generic mechanism until then.
- Terminology placed over these mechanisms is decided once the Combat Mindset Framework is formally settled, so the design must allow terminology to be swapped late.

## 4. Minimum information required before Phase 2 can begin

1. **The TAD training programme / timetable** (Q1), including the Lead Self lesson serials, the timing of Ex One and Ex Two, field and other pressured activity, existing debrief/AAR points, and march-out.
2. **An answer on re-sequencing freedom** (Q2), which normally comes with the timetable.

Everything else can proceed on the recorded design assumptions (see `07`) and be confirmed during Phase 2.

**Strongly recommended, but not blocking:** request Q4 (assessment) and Q6 (standard LDS tools) at the same time as the timetable. Either answer could constrain the evidence system. For example, if the LDP is formally assessed in a fixed format, or if Keep/Stop/Start frequency is an LDS requirement, Phase 2 would need to rework those parts.
