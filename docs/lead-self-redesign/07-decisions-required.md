## Settled

### D3. Development structures and learner framing — **SETTLED**

Three structures, connected not combined:

| Structure | Sequence |
|---|---|
| NZALC development method | LEARN → PRACTICE → PRESSURE → APPLY → REINFORCE |
| Recruit self-development loop | EXPERIENCE → FEEDBACK → REFLECT → ADAPT → REAPPLY ↺ (REAPPLY becomes the next EXPERIENCE) |
| Pressure thread (not a third loop) | PREPARE → PERFORM → RECOVER |

- The learner-facing framing is **"Your Lead Self Journey"**, supported by five questions: *Who am I? How do I affect others? How do I perform? What does the evidence tell me? What will I change?* These are **not** five mandatory chapters.
- "Orientate" is not used as a stage label.
- This supersedes the nine-step spine in doc 04 as a single model.
- Owner note (not blocking): NZALC to confirm the source for the development-method wording (08, Q11).

### D5-a. CM / PUP design approach — **SETTLED**

- Unsettled terminology and doctrine are not resolved here. Source terminology and distinctions are preserved.
- Only source-supported integration points are built: recognise interference; maintain situational awareness; regulate response; refocus attention; act; review performance (sources in 08 §1.3).
- No WIN without an authoritative source.
- Terminology must be swappable late.

### D14. Resilience definition — **SETTLED**

Retain the workbook definition ("the capacity to perform under and recover from adversity", p.57) until CM Framework terminology is approved. Track as a dependent change.

# DESIGN BRIEF FOR PHASE 2

*Phase 2 has not begun. It starts once D2/4 is answered. This brief reflects the design position in 08.*

## Purpose

Design the **developmental architecture** of the Lead Self workbook **around the TAD course**, not the existing chapter order. It covers how the three structures (D3) connect, where the recruit loop turns, the evidence trail, and the placement of every existing component. **Phase 2 does not design pages.** Its output is a structure NZALC can review and approve before learner-facing design (Phase 4) or prototyping (Phase 5).

## Fixed constraints

1. Course aim and LO 1.1–1.3: verbatim.
2. Leadership Framework, Value Adds, Key Elements, Essential Tasks, Behaviour Statements, Mission/Vision/Ethos/Values and te reo: verbatim. Only placement and presentation can change.
3. The three structures in D3 are **connected, not combined**. The pressure thread is not a development loop.
4. The exercise → self/peer feedback → summary → LDP cycle is the load-bearing structure. Ex One and Ex Two remain the major evidence points (DR-3).
5. DEB/DESC and SSR formats are kept.
6. DR-1, DR-2 and DR-4 are met.
7. No invented doctrine, including CM/PUP. Gaps are flagged, not filled. Terminology is swappable (D5-a).

## What Phase 2 must produce

| # | Output | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Course map** | The TAD programme annotated with where recruits actually experience pressure, teamwork, failure, feedback, field activity and opportunities to reapply behaviour |
| 2 | **Structure connection** | Where the NZALC method, the recruit loop and the pressure thread each operate across the course, and how they hand off to each other |
| 3 | **Loop turns** | Each turn of EXPERIENCE → FEEDBACK → REFLECT → ADAPT → REAPPLY placed on the course map, including the early low-stakes feedback point (DR-2) and the between-exercise reapply mechanism and frequency (D17) |
| 4 | **Component placement** | Every KEEP/MODIFY/MERGE/MOVE component from doc 02 placed on the course map, tagged to Value Add(s) and Behaviour Statements |
| 5 | **Evidence trail spec** | Inputs, outputs and flow from experience to reassessment, including the Identity vs Reputation thread from baseline to march-out |
| 6 | **Pressure / performance integration points** | Where the six source-supported integration points appear, with sources cited and labels swappable |
| 7 | **Traceability matrix** | LO → Value Add → component → evidence produced |
| 8 | **Interactive "Architecture Lab"** (optional) | An HTML tool for placing content blocks on the course map and seeing coverage update |

## Design principles

- **Soldier-centred and plain-language.** Written for an 18-year-old recruit.
- **Behaviour-focused.** Reflections ask about observable actions and words.
- **Evidence over opinion.** Self-view is always tested against events and others' observations.
- **Every plan is reapplied and reviewed.** No plan is created without a point where it is tried again and checked (DR-1).
- **Identity vs Reputation is experienced, not just taught** (DR-2).
- **No new administrative burden.** Attach to existing training (DR-4).
- **Pressure is the environment, not a chapter.**
- **Value Adds are visible throughout.**
- **Doctrinal accuracy over design novelty.**

## Success test for Phase 2

The architecture is ready for Phase 4 when NZALC can answer **yes** to each:

1. Can we trace any line in a recruit's final LDP back to a specific event and specific feedback?
2. Does the recruit reapply their mid-course priority and receive external evidence before the next major review?
3. Does the recruit get evidence about the identity–reputation gap early, well before Ex One?
4. Does every LO have evidence produced at least twice?
5. Does every existing doctrinal element have a home?
6. Would a section commander recognise the TAD programme in it, without finding a new weekly form?
7. Can the PUP/CM terminology be changed without restructuring?

## Out of scope for Phase 2

Page layouts, visual design, HTML prototypes of learner pages (Phase 4–5), rewriting doctrinal text, instructor lesson plans, and resolving Combat Mindset terminology.
