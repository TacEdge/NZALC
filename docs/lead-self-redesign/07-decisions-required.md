# 07 — Decisions Required Before Redesign

*NZALC should make these calls before Phase 2 begins. Each has a recommendation, but the decision belongs to NZALC. They are grouped by how much they constrain everything else.*

---

## Tier 1 — Foundational (decide first; everything else depends on them)

### D1. Learning Outcomes and assessment

**Question.** Are LO 1.1–1.3 and the course aim fixed? How are the LOs assessed at PL 1, and is any workbook content assessed or inspected?

**Why it matters.** The evidence system (doc 06) must produce whatever the assessment regime needs. If the LDP is formally assessed, its fields are an assessment instrument.

**Recommendation.** Confirm the LOs are fixed (the audit assumes so). Supply the assessment specification or the COG 1 training documentation.

### D2. TAD delivery timetable

**Question.** When is each Lead Self lesson delivered relative to Exercise One, Exercise Two and march-out? How long is the course?

**Why it matters.** The journey analysis (doc 03) follows workbook order because no timetable was supplied. Options A and C (doc 04) cannot be finalised without it. For example: is Resilience really taught last? Is Intent taught before or after first parade?

**Recommendation.** Supply the current Lead Self serial/timetable within the TAD programme.

### D3. The developmental spine and its language

**Question.** Is the nine-step spine the internal design scaffold, the learner-facing structure, or neither? How does it relate to LEARN → PRACTICE → PRESSURE → APPLY → REINFORCE and to Prepare–Perform–Recover?

**Options.**

- **a.** Nine-step spine as internal design scaffold only. Learner sees a simpler loop.
- **b.** NZALC five-step as learner-facing language, nine-step internal.
- **c.** Prepare–Perform–Recover as the learner-facing loop (already NZALC doctrine per CN/FP), with the nine-step as the design scaffold.

**Recommendation.** **(a) or (c).** Recruits need one short loop they can remember. Nine steps is a designer's tool. Rename the "ORIENTATE" step to avoid collision with the LDS *Orientate* phase.

### D4. Organising principle and freedom to re-sequence

**Question.** Topic-led (Option A), Value Add-led (Option B) or cycle-led (Option C)? Can NZALC change the lesson delivery sequence, or must the workbook follow the existing TAD serials?

**Recommendation.** **Option C with Value Add tagging**, if re-sequencing is possible (at minimum, moving the pressure/mental-skills material early). Otherwise **Option A with Value Add tagging** and a reassessment loop.

### D5. Combat Mindset: terminology, status and sources

**Questions.**

1. What term should the workbook use? "Combat Mindset Conditioning" does not appear in any supplied source. The drafts use *Combat Mindset*, *Performance Under Pressure* and *Combat Mindset Framework*.
2. Should the Lead Self redesign wait for, run alongside, or serve as a pilot for the Combat Mindset Framework programme (FP Phases 3–4, Oct–Nov 2026)?
3. Is **WIN / What's Important Now** to be included? If so, what is the authoritative source?
4. Can NZALC supply the **PUP Workbook**, which defines Red Head–Blue Head and details Prepare–Perform–Recover and mental-skills practice?

**Why it matters.** Under FP's recommended Option 1, most Lead Self content is PUP, not Combat Mindset (doc 05 §5). Adopting CM language now risks reprinting later.

**Recommendation.**

- Use **Performance Under Pressure** language (already NZALC-owned and consistent with CN's "Lead Self: perform under pressure").
- Design so terminology can be swapped once the Framework is approved.
- Propose the Lead Self redesign to the Framework programme as a **bounded pilot** (both CN rec. 7 and FP Phase 4 call for one).
- Do **not** include WIN until a source is supplied.

## Tier 2 — Design-shaping

### D6. Move pressure material to the front?

**Question.** Should Comfort Zone, P = P – I, a small set of mental skills and the SSR be introduced in the foundations, rather than in Ch 2 (comfort zone) and Ch 12 (the rest)?

**Recommendation.** **Yes.** Recruits face pressure from week one, and the tools are most useful before exposure, not after. The Resilience lesson content itself can stay where the timetable puts it, with the tools previewed early.

### D7. Which reflection instruments are mandated?

**Question.** Are DEB/DESC, Keep/Stop/Start, SSR, Strength/Development Area rating and the hot-seat feedback round mandated NZALC/LDS methods (e.g. common across Lead Teams, Lead Leaders, LSYS workbooks), or local choices?

**Why it matters.** Doc 06 proposes reducing K/S/S from ~27 to ~4–6 uses and replacing chapter ratings with a lighter "Try-it". If these tools are LDS-standard, the redesign should keep them and change *how often* they appear, not *whether*.

**Recommendation.** Keep DEB/DESC, SSR and the feedback round unchanged in format. Limit K/S/S to LDPs and game plans. Replace chapter-level rating-plus-K/S/S with "Try-it", unless it is mandated.

### D8. What were the twin Lesson Reflection pages for?

**Question.** Is "Lesson Reflection 2" intended as a later re-rating, a spare, or something else?

**Recommendation.** If re-rating is intended, show it as a second column on one page. If it is a spare, remove it. Either way, state the purpose on the page.

### D9. Instructor and section commander observation

**Question.** Should instructor or section commander observations enter the evidence trail (e.g. one DEB/DESC observation per recruit per exercise, or input at the LDP review)?

**Why it matters.** Currently only self and peer perspectives exist. CN lists "observation of behaviour" as part of the NZALC methodology. Instructor input materially improves evidence quality for LO 1.1 and 1.3, but adds instructor workload.

**Recommendation.** Include one instructor observation per exercise cycle as an input to the LDP, if instructor capacity allows. Decide with TAD.

### D10. Mental health continuum

**Questions.** Is the Canadian Forces continuum still the endorsed model? Does Army Psychology Services own this content? Should the "where do you sit now?" prompt be written in a workbook peers may see?

**Recommendation.** Keep the content (duty of care). Make the self-location private or unwritten with a clear help-seeking pathway. Confirm currency with APS.

## Tier 3 — Implementation and governance

### D12. Format, ownership and privacy

**Questions.**

- Print workbook only, or print plus digital/HTML?
- Does the recruit keep everything? The workbook says "yours to keep".
- Are peer sheets torn out of the workbook or issued separately?
- What do instructors see?

**Recommendation.** Decide the format before Phase 4 page design. Keep peer sheets separate from the bound workbook to avoid destroying backing pages. Define a simple privacy rule: *mine / shared with my section / seen by instructors*.

### D13. Cultural authority

**Question.** Who approves any re-presentation of the Tōtara framework story, te reo Māori names and related content?

**Recommendation.** No change to wording. Any layout change is reviewed by the appropriate NZDF/Army cultural adviser.

### D14. Terminology alignment

**Question.** The workbook defines resilience as "the capacity to perform under and recover from adversity". CN proposes "the capacity to absorb adversity, continue functioning, adapt and recover".

**Recommendation.** Retain the workbook definition until the Combat Mindset Framework terminology is approved. Track it as a dependent change.

### D15. Transfer beyond march-out

**Question.** Should the final LDP be handed over to the next phase of training or unit (e.g. an IET section commander), or does it remain the recruit's property only?

**Why it matters.** CN places reinforcement responsibility on force employers ("reinforce common language… observe leadership behaviour; provide feedback"). Without handover, REINFORCE ends at march-out.

**Recommendation.** At minimum, include a one-line "first 90 days in my next unit" commitment. Explore a handover with IET schools.

### D16. Doctrine currency check

**Question.** Are the NZDDP 0.06 references, Value Add text and artwork (pp.2–4) and Behaviour Statements current as of 2026?

**Recommendation.** Confirm with the doctrine owner before Phase 2 reuses any text.

# DESIGN BRIEF FOR PHASE 2

*This is the brief for Phase 2 work. Phase 2 has not begun.*

## Purpose

Design the **developmental architecture** of the Lead Self workbook: the sequence, the development loop, the evidence trail and the placement of every existing component. **Phase 2 does not design pages.** Its output is a structure NZALC can review and approve before any learner-facing design (Phase 4) or prototyping (Phase 5).

## Fixed constraints

1. Course aim and LO 1.1–1.3: verbatim.
2. Leadership Framework, Value Adds, Key Elements, Essential Tasks, Behaviour Statements, Mission/Vision/Ethos/Values and te reo: verbatim. Only placement and presentation can change.
3. The exercise → self/peer feedback → summary → LDP cycle is kept as the load-bearing structure.
4. DEB/DESC and SSR formats are kept.
5. No invented doctrine, including CMC. Gaps are flagged, not filled.

## What Phase 2 must produce

| # | Output | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Course arc** | Foundations → development cycles → carry forward, placed against the TAD timetable (D2) |
| 2 | **Development loop** | The repeating loop (per D3 language), with what happens at each step at weekly and exercise scale |
| 3 | **Component placement** | Every KEEP/MODIFY/MERGE/MOVE component from doc 02 placed in the arc, tagged to Value Add(s) and Behaviour Statements |
| 4 | **Evidence system spec** | The five tools (Baseline, Try-it, Pressure debrief, Feedback, Plan & review) and the Development Record: inputs, outputs, frequency, and what feeds what |
| 5 | **PUP / CM thread** | Where P = P – I, the zone check, mental skills and SSR appear across the arc, cited to approved sources only, with swappable terminology (D5) |
| 6 | **Traceability matrix** | LO → Value Add → component → evidence produced, showing every LO is evidenced |
| 7 | **Interactive "Architecture Lab"** (optional, per tasking) | An HTML tool in which NZALC can drag existing content blocks onto the arc and see Value Add / LO / spine coverage update, before committing to a structure |

## Design principles (from tasking, sharpened by audit)

- **Soldier-centred and plain-language.** Written for an 18-year-old recruit, not for leadership theorists.
- **Behaviour-focused.** Every reflection asks about observable actions and words, not feelings about competence.
- **Evidence over opinion.** Self-ratings are always paired with, or tested against, events and others' observations.
- **Differentiated reflection.** Each tool has one job and one question (doc 04 §1, doc 06 §4). No two consecutive reflections should look the same.
- **Few tools, used often.** Five recognisable tools beat nine sporadic ones.
- **Every plan is reviewed.** No plan is created without a scheduled point where it is checked.
- **Pressure is the environment, not a chapter.** Pressure tools go early and recur.
- **Value Adds are visible throughout.** Every topic states which Value Add it builds.
- **Doctrinal accuracy over design novelty.**

## Success test for Phase 2

The architecture is ready for Phase 4 when NZALC can answer **yes** to each:

1. Can we trace any line in a recruit's final LDP back to a specific event and to specific feedback?
2. Does every LO have evidence produced at least twice across the course?
3. Is there a point where the recruit checks whether their mid-course plan worked?
4. Does every existing doctrinal element have a home?
5. Would a section commander recognise the course timetable in it?
6. Can the PUP/CM terminology be changed without restructuring?

## Out of scope for Phase 2

Page layouts, visual design, HTML prototypes of learner pages (Phase 4–5), rewriting doctrinal text, instructor lesson plans.
