# 01 — Current-State Map

*Phase 1 audit of the Lead Self Leadership Development Workbook (TAD), Version April 2026. Page references are printed page numbers (PDF page = p.N + 7).*

---

## 1. What the workbook says it is for

**Course context (p.ii).** NZ Army Recruit Training: *"To produce a combat focused Band One soldier, capable of operating as a section member in an RF All Arms Section."*

**Course aim (p.ii).** The course is part of the Leadership Development System (LDS). Its aim is to help Regular Force personnel enlisting into the NZDF to:

- enhance strategic self-awareness;
- explore NZDF expectations at the Lead Self level IAW the NZDF Leadership Framework;
- develop a personalised Leadership Development Plan (LDP).

**Learning Outcomes (p.ii).** COG 1 LDS Lead Self – RF:

| LO | Statement | Level |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Demonstrate Self Awareness as a Lead Self Leader | PL 1 |
| 1.2 | Add Value as a Lead Self Leader | PL 1 |
| 1.3 | Create a Personalised Leadership Development Plan (LDP) as a Lead Self Leader | PL 1 |

**Doctrinal anchors the workbook uses:**

- **Leadership definition** (NZDDP 0.06 para 1.11), self-awareness, situational awareness, strategic self-awareness (NZDDP 0.06 p.63), all on p.1.
- **The NZDF Leadership Framework**: the Tōtara tree, six Key Elements (p.2), Growth Rings / Te Pūtake (p.3), Orientate–Build–Advance (p.3).
- **Four Lead Self Value Adds (Taki Tūwā)** (p.4): Follow Leader's Intent (Whai Hau Tū), Apply Good Judgement (Taituara), Support Others (Whakaaro Nui), Pursue Excellence (Whai Hiranga).
- **Key Elements, Essential Tasks and Behaviour Statements** (pp.5–6).
- **NZDF/Army Mission, Vision, Ethos and Values** (pp.16–17).

## 4. Behaviour Statement coverage in the chapter reflections

Each chapter reflection asks the recruit to rate five Behaviour Statements. That is 55 ratings in total, of which about 45 statements are unique. **Ten statements recur across two chapters.** Examples:

| Statement | Chapters |
|---|---|
| Be willing to change your thinking patterns and behaviour | Ethos & Values; Above/Below the Line |
| Behave in a predictable, consistent and fair manner | Above/Below the Line; Trust |
| Admit mistakes and ask for help when needed | Lead Self Dev; Trust |
| Speak up against behaviour that does not meet the NZDF Ethos and Values | Ethos & Values; Conflict |
| Stand by the Ethos and Values when these are challenged | Ethos & Values; Culture |
| Respect others' perspectives and remain open to new ideas and methods | Trust; Culture |
| Speak and act in ways others understand | Trust; Culture |
| Accept accountability for your actions, outputs and behaviours | Above/Below the Line; Intent |

This recurrence may be deliberate. It is the only place the workbook implicitly connects topics. But the recruit is never told so, and the ratings are never compared.

### Chapter → Value Add alignment (implicit; not stated in the workbook)

| Value Add | Chapters whose reflection statements mainly map to it |
|---|---|
| **Support Others** | Ethos & Values, Above/Below the Line, Trust, Conflict, Team Development, Culture (+ part Resilience) |
| **Follow Leader's Intent** | Intent (+ part Above/Below the Line) |
| **Apply Good Judgement** | Judgement and Decisions |
| **Pursue Excellence** | Lead Self Development, Change, Resilience |

The LDP asks the recruit to tag each priority to a Value Add. **The chapters never tell the recruit which Value Add they serve.** The recruit has to make that mapping alone at the moment of planning.
