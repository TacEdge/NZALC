# 10 — Validation Plan

*A lightweight check of the assumptions under ST-4 (Section Commander sustainability) and ST-5 (recruit use after Day 2), before Phase 2 is locked. This is not formal research, and it is not a test of whether people like the redesign.*

**Decision context.** A1 (core architecture) is **provisionally accepted, pending lightweight user validation** (09). Validation should identify **adjustments**. It should trigger redesign only where evidence **materially contradicts** an assumption (§6).

---

## 1. Purpose

Find out, from people who have been through it, whether the architecture's two human assumptions hold:

- **ST-4.** A Corporal can sustain Lead Self development through normal section leadership, without it becoming administration.
- **ST-5.** A recruit would have a genuine reason to use a development tool after Day 2, and could realistically have it to hand.

Also gather the facts needed to choose a Part 2 format (05 §4). That decision is deliberately left open until then.

---

---

## 3. Approach

| Item | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **Who** | About 3–5 recent recruits (recently completed TAD, or late in the course) and 3–5 current or recent TAD Section Commanders |
| **How** | Ten-minute informal conversations, one to one. Not focus groups: peers can shape each other's answers, and recruits may not speak freely about Section Commanders in a group |
| **Who asks** | Someone who is **not** the interviewee's Section Commander or in their chain of command, where possible |
| **Order** | Ask about what actually happened **before** describing anything about the redesign. Show the proposed Section Commander behaviours only at the end of the Section Commander conversation (SC-7) |
| **Wording** | Ask about specific past events ("the last time…", "what happened when…"), not opinions about hypotheticals. Avoid naming the answer in the question |
| **Approval** | With the appropriate chain-of-command agreement. No names recorded against answers |
| **Optional extra** | Look at 3–5 used workbooks from a recent intake: which pages are filled in, and where completion stops |

---

## 4. Questions

Follow-up prompts are in italics. Use them only if the answer is thin.

### 4.1 Recent recruits

| # | Question | Tests |
|---|---|---|
| **R-1** | "Think back to the Lead Self days in week one. What happened to the workbook after those two days?" *Where did it end up? When did you last see it?* | AS-1, AS-3 |
| **R-2** | "Was there ever a time after those days that you opened it? Tell me about that." *What made you open it? If never, what would have needed to happen?* | AS-1 |
| **R-3** | "During training, was there any time you thought about how you were coming across to your section, or how you could do better? What prompted that?" *Did anyone else prompt it? What did you do with the thought?* | AS-1, AS-10 |
| **R-4** | "What did you actually carry on you, or have easy access to, during a normal training day? And in the field?" *Notebook? Anything in pockets? When could you get to your locker?* | AS-3, AS-2 |
| **R-5** | "Was there a part of the day when you had a few minutes to yourself, or with your section, that wasn't taken up?" *What usually happened then?* | AS-2, AS-9 |
| **R-6** | "Tell me about a time someone in your section gave you feedback on how you were doing. What was it like?" *Was it useful? What made it useful or not? Did you change anything?* | AS-4 |
| **R-7** | "How did it feel when your Section Commander gave you feedback that wasn't about a mark or a pass/fail?" *Did it feel different from being assessed? Did you say what you really thought?* | AS-5 |
| **R-8** | "Looking back, what, if anything, made the Lead Self material feel connected to the rest of training? What made it feel separate?" | AS-1, overall |

### 4.2 Current or recent Section Commanders

| # | Question | Tests |
|---|---|---|
| **SC-1** | "Walk me through how you delivered the two Lead Self days last time." *What worked? What did recruits engage with? What did you skip or shorten, and why?* | Context; two-day design |
| **SC-2** | "After those two days, what happened to Lead Self in your section?" *Did it come up again? When? Who brought it up?* | AS-1, AS-6 |
| **SC-3** | "Did you see recruits using the workbook after week one? Did you use it yourself?" *When? For what?* | AS-1, AS-3 |
| **SC-4** | "What happens after Exercise One and Exercise Two in terms of feedback to recruits? Who runs it and how long does it take?" *Is there a section feedback session now? Does the workbook come into it?* | **AS-7** |
| **SC-5** | "Tell me about the last time you helped a recruit improve something about how they behave in the section, not a skill." *How did you go about it? When did that conversation happen?* | AS-6, AS-8 |
| **SC-6** | "In a normal day, when are the moments you'd naturally have a quick word with an individual recruit, or with the section?" *End of day? After AARs? During admin time?* | AS-9 |
| **SC-7** | *(Now show the five working-minimum behaviours, 04 §2.)* "If you were asked to do these, which would you already be doing anyway? Which, if any, would feel like extra admin?" *What would make any of them unrealistic?* | **AS-6, AS-8, ST-4** |
| **SC-8** | "Do recruits treat feedback from you differently from being assessed by you? How can you tell?" *Do they open up or close down?* | AS-5 |

**Deliberately not asked:** "Would you like a pocket booklet?", "Is this redesign better?", or any question that describes the proposed format.

## 5. What would confirm or challenge each test

### ST-4 — Section Commander sustainability

| Evidence that would **confirm** | Evidence that would **challenge** |
|---|---|
| Section Commanders describe already coaching and questioning individual recruits about behaviour (SC-5) | Section Commanders describe little or no individual behavioural coaching, or no moments to do it |
| Section Commanders identify existing moments where a quick question fits (SC-6) | No identifiable moments; days are fully consumed |
| A post-exercise feedback process already exists and Section Commanders run it (SC-4) | No post-exercise feedback process exists, or it is run by someone else, so facilitating rounds would be **additional** |
| Section Commanders say most of the five behaviours are "things I'd do anyway" (SC-7) | Two or more of the five are seen as extra admin by most Section Commanders |
| Recruits report Section Commander feedback as useful and reasonably safe (R-7, SC-8) | Recruits consistently report Section Commander feedback as indistinguishable from assessment, or say they "say what they want to hear" |

### ST-5 — Recruit use after Day 2

| Evidence that would **confirm** | Evidence that would **challenge** |
|---|---|
| Recruits say the workbook went unused because nobody asked and it never connected to anything (R-1, R-2, R-8). This is consistent with H1/H5/H6 | Recruits say it went unused purely because of lack of time or access, even if someone had asked |
| Recruits report moments of self-reflection prompted by people or events (R-3). The behaviour exists and needs a hook | No self-reflection reported at all, even informally |
| Recruits can name something they reliably carry or reach (R-4) | Nothing is reliably carried or reachable during training |
| Peer feedback described as useful when specific (R-6) | Peer feedback described as unhelpful, unsafe or socially risky regardless of format |
| Some routine slack time exists (R-5) | No slack time at all outside Section Commander-directed activity |
| Used workbooks show completion stopping right after the Lead Self days (optional) | Workbooks show continued use, which would mean the problem lies elsewhere |

## 6. What evidence would change the architecture

Changes are graded. **Adjust** keeps the architecture and tunes a component. **Redesign** changes a structural element and is triggered only by material contradiction.

| If validation finds… | Then… | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Post-exercise feedback rounds don't currently exist or aren't run by Section Commanders (AS-7 false) | Re-scope the major intensity. Fold feedback into an existing post-exercise AAR, or make the round recruit-led with a short Section Commander contribution. Re-test ST-4 | **Adjust** (Redesign if no post-exercise slot exists at all) |
| Most Section Commanders see two or more of the five behaviours as extra admin | Cut the minimum to what they already do. Shift the burden to buddy pairs and the tool. Re-test ST-4 | **Adjust** |
| Section Commanders report no individual coaching moments at all in TAD (AS-6, AS-8, AS-9 false) | The Section Commander is not a viable continuity mechanism for everyday intensity. Redesign the everyday loop around buddy pairs and existing section-level activity, and keep the Section Commander role only at the major points | **Redesign** (of Section Commander role) |
| Section Commander feedback is consistently experienced as assessment (AS-5 false) | Strengthen the separation in 04 §5.1. Lean on peer feedback for development, and use Section Commander input mainly at major points. Resolve D1/16 accordingly | **Adjust** |
| Recruits carry nothing reliably and have no reachable moment (AS-2, AS-3 false) | Part 2 cannot depend on a carried item. Make the priority live in conversation and memory, with the written record only at exercise points (FR-3, FR-7, FR-8). Choose a format accordingly | **Adjust** (format) |
| Workbook non-use is driven by time alone, even when prompted (AS-1 false) | The social-trigger premise of ST-5 is weakened. Reduce Part 2 to exercise-point use only. Rely on conversation for everyday intensity | **Adjust** |
| Recruits report no self-reflection of any kind during TAD, and peer feedback as unsafe regardless of format (AS-4, AS-10 false) | The recruit-owned loop premise is materially contradicted. Revisit the balance between recruit ownership and Section Commander-led review before Phase 3 | **Redesign** |
| Recruits find one priority too narrow, or say multiple areas are always raised | Allow one *active* priority plus a short "noted for later" list. Keep one-at-a-time for reapplication | **Adjust** |
| Findings broadly confirm the assumptions | Lock Phase 2 (A1 → SETTLED). Choose the Part 2 format from R-4 / SC-3 / SC-6 evidence | — |

**What would not change the architecture on its own:** preferences about appearance, length or colour; a single outlier interview; requests for more content.

## 7. How findings should be recorded

Record one row per conversation. No names; role and approximate timing only. Keep notes close to what was said. Quote briefly where it is telling.

### 7.1 Conversation log template

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| ID | R1, R2… / SC1, SC2… |
| Role and context | e.g. "recruit, completed TAD ~2 months ago" / "Section Commander, two intakes" |
| Date | |
| Key answers by question | R-1 … R-8 / SC-1 … SC-8 (short notes) |
| Telling quotes | 1–3 short quotes |
| Assumptions supported | AS-# |
| Assumptions challenged | AS-# |
| Surprises / new issues | Anything the architecture hasn't considered |

### 7.2 Synthesis (after all conversations)

| Assumption | Supported by | Challenged by | Verdict (Holds / Mixed / Fails) | Consequence (from §6) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AS-1 … AS-10 | IDs | IDs | | |

Then:

1. Re-score **ST-4** and **ST-5** (08) as PASS / CONDITIONAL / FAIL, with reasons.
2. List the **adjustments** to make, and any **redesign** triggered.
3. Record the **Part 2 format facts**: what recruits carry, when they can reach it, where a priority would naturally live.
4. Propose A1 → SETTLED, or state what still blocks it.

Store the log and synthesis as `11-validation-findings.md` in this folder. Feed it back for the Phase 2 lock.
