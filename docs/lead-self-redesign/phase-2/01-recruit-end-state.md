# 01 — Recruit End State (end of Day 2)

*What a recruit should leave the two Section Commander-delivered days with. The governing outcomes are LO 1.1–1.3 and the Lead Self Behaviour Statements. No new standard is introduced.*

---

## 1. The governing outcomes

| LO | Statement |
|---|---|
| 1.1 | Demonstrate Self Awareness as a Lead Self Leader |
| 1.2 | Add Value as a Lead Self Leader |
| 1.3 | Create a Personalised Leadership Development Plan (LDP) as a Lead Self Leader |

### The development loop is already in the doctrine

The Lead Self Behaviour Statements (workbook pp.5–6) and Value Add text (p.4) already describe the recruit self-development loop. The loop is therefore an expression of existing standards, not an addition to them.

| Loop step | Existing doctrinal text | Source |
|---|---|---|
| EXPERIENCE | "Recognise stressful situations (training) as an opportunity to learn and grow." | IO: Develop the Resilience of Others |
| FEEDBACK | "Self Leaders actively seek feedback to improve, and consider diverse perspectives." / "Give others feedback and assistance." / "They seek coaching, and provide honest feedback about their own and others' performance and impact on the team." | FLI Value Add; IO: Build Trust; PE Value Add |
| REFLECT | "Regularly reflect on your behaviour & performance to become self-aware." / "…regularly reflecting on their behaviour to understand and manage the impact they have on those around them." | IO: Model Self-Awareness and Self Control; SO Value Add |
| ADAPT | "Identify and set development goals." / "Be willing to change your thinking patterns and behaviour." | IO: Model Self-Awareness; DPC: Embrace Coaching and Mentoring |
| REAPPLY | "Apply lessons learned to future actions and decisions." / "Use mistakes as opportunities to learn." | TS (Think Smart) |
| (Review) | "Assess your performance against development goals." *(printed "Asses", p.5)* | IO: Model Self-Awareness |
| (Ownership) | "Acknowledge that personal and professional development is essential and you must own this." | DPC: Embrace Coaching and Mentoring |

**Design consequence.** When a Section Commander asks "how did your priority go today?", they are reinforcing a Behaviour Statement, not running an extra process.

---

## 2. The end-state statement (08 §1.5)

> "I understand what effective Lead Self behaviour looks like. I have observed some of my own behaviour. I have received evidence from another person. I have reflected on that evidence. I have identified an initial development priority. I know what I will deliberately do differently. I know how to reapply that behaviour during recruit training. I know how to use subsequent experience and feedback to continue developing myself."

The tables below break that statement into separate, testable elements. **Knowledge** (what they can recall or explain) is kept apart from **observable capability** (what a Section Commander could see them do).

|---|---|---|

| K2 | The four Lead Self Value Adds by name: Follow Leader's Intent, Apply Good Judgement, Support Others, Pursue Excellence | 1.2 |
| K3 | Identity = how I see me; reputation = how others see me; reputation comes from the consequences of observable behaviour | 1.1 |
| K4 | The difference between helpful and hindering behaviour (Above / Below the Line) | 1.2 |

### 3.2 UNDERSTANDING (can explain why)

| # | The recruit understands… | LO |
|---|---|---|
| U1 | Why feedback is needed: you can't fully see your own reputation | 1.1 |
| U2 | Why the Value Adds matter in a section: each describes a way they make the section more effective | 1.2 |
| U3 | Why behaviour changes under pressure, and that this is normal and trainable | 1.1 |
| U4 | Why development is their responsibility, and how the Section Commander and buddy support it rather than run it | 1.3 |

### 3.3 HAVING EXPERIENCED

| # | The recruit has… | Where (02) |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Taken part in a real section task while being observed | Day 1 |
| E2 | Received specific feedback from a peer and the Section Commander on that task | Day 1 |
| E3 | Noticed a gap, or a match, between what they intended and what others saw | Day 1 |

**E5 matters most.** It means the recruit has completed one full turn of the loop before the method is formally handed over.

### 3.4 BEING ABLE TO DO (observable capability)

Each item says what a Section Commander would see. These are candidate indicators for LO evidence (subject to D1/16).

| # | Capability | What the Section Commander would observe | LO | Behaviour Statement anchor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Describe their own behaviour in observable terms | States what they *did and said*, not just how they felt | 1.1 | Regularly reflect on your behaviour & performance |
| C2 | Give useful feedback | Gives a peer one specific strength and one development observation, each with an example (DEB/DESC structure) | 1.2 | Give others feedback and assistance |
| C3 | Receive feedback well | Listens, asks a clarifying question, does not argue or excuse, thanks the giver | 1.1 | Accept questions & input… / Admit mistakes and ask for help |
| C4 | Compare intention with impact | Names one difference (or match) between how they meant to come across and what others observed | 1.1 | Model Self-Awareness |
| C5 | Recognise their own signs of interference | Names one personal early sign that pressure is affecting them | 1.1 | Stay calm & rational in stressful settings |
| C6 | Use a regulation and a refocus technique | Demonstrates tactical breathing; uses a refocus technique (e.g. instructional self-talk) during the pressure task | 1.1 | Practice resilience skills to stay calm |
| C7 | Run a short pressure debrief | Answers: what happened, what I did, what worked, what next | 1.1 | Use mistakes as opportunities to learn |
| C8 | Write a development priority as a behaviour | States one priority as an observable behaviour, tied to a situation and a Value Add (e.g. "When we're under time pressure, I will check my team before acting") | **1.3** | Identify and set development goals |
| C9 | Explain how they will reapply and get evidence | Names when they expect to use it next, and who will tell them how it went (buddy, Section Commander) | 1.3 | Assess your performance against development goals |

### 3.5 HAVING RECEIVED AS EVIDENCE

By the end of Day 2, each recruit holds at least:

| # | Evidence | From |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | Their own identity statement ("how I want my section to experience me") | Self, Day 1 |
| V2 | One or more peer observations from the Day 1 task | Buddy / peers |
| V3 | One Section Commander observation | Section Commander (minimum one, can be brief) |
| V4 | A peer observation of their Day 2 reapply attempt | Buddy |
| V5 | Their own pressure debrief from Day 2 | Self |

This is the start of the evidence thread that leads to the LDP (05).

### 3.6 HAVING COMMITTED TO

| # | Commitment |
|---|---|
| M1 | **One** development priority (C8), carried visibly |
| M2 | A named buddy and how they will check in |
| M3 | A values commitment: how they will show the Army values in their section (retained from workbook p.17) |
| M4 | A section commitment: how the section wants to be seen at march-out (retained from workbook p.44) |

Only **one** personal priority, deliberately. The current workbook produces 11+ development areas that are never carried forward. One priority, carried and checked, is the core change.

### 3.7 KNOWING HOW TO CONTINUE

| # | The recruit knows… |
|---|---|
| N1 | That the loop repeats in everyday training, and what each step means for them (03) |
| N2 | That their Section Commander will ask about their priority at natural moments, and what a good answer looks like |

## 4. What the recruit is NOT expected to leave Day 2 with

To protect the two days from becoming a cramming exercise (risk R4):

- full recall of the Leadership Framework, Essential Tasks or all Behaviour Statements (these become reference);
- mastery of every model in the current workbook (Schein, OODA, the Five-Step Process, the risk matrix, functional leadership);
- a full LDP. A full LDP is built from Ex One evidence. Day 2 produces the **first priority**, which seeds it;
- self-ratings against every Behaviour Statement.
