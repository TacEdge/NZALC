# 07 — Pressure / Performance Integration

*Places the six agreed, source-supported mechanisms across the system. Combat Mindset terminology is not resolved. WIN is not introduced. Prepare → Perform → Recover is a pressure frame, not a development cycle (08 §1.7).*

**Sources.** WB = Lead Self Workbook (TAD) 2026. CN = Draft Capability Note (draft, not endorsed). Labels below are **neutral working labels** and can be swapped once the Combat Mindset Framework is settled.

## 3. The pressure thread around a pressured activity

The same three-word frame is used around the Day 2 pressure task, Ex One, Ex Two and (optionally) any hard day:

| Frame | Recruit does | Mechanisms |
|---|---|---|
| **PREPARE** | Recall my priority. Pick one tool. Know my early signs | M1 (anticipate), M3/M4 (choose tool) |
| **PERFORM** | Notice → regulate → refocus → check SA → act | M1–M5 |
| **RECOVER** | What happened? What did I do? What worked? What next? Get feedback | M6, which feeds REFLECT and ADAPT in the recruit loop |

**How the pressure thread connects to the recruit loop** (connected, not combined):

```
Recruit loop:   EXPERIENCE ─────────────── FEEDBACK ── REFLECT ── ADAPT ── REAPPLY ↺
                    │                         ▲
Pressure frame:  PREPARE → PERFORM → RECOVER ─┘
                 (only when the EXPERIENCE is a pressured one)
```

Recovery hands into reflection. The pressure frame never replaces the loop.

---

## 4. Combat-specificity note

Under the CM Framework proposal's recommended Option 1 (not yet approved), most of this is **Performance Under Pressure** (the enabling capability). Exercise One and Exercise Two may count as combat-specific only if they deliberately simulate threat, significant consequence and ethical load (Phase 1 doc 05 §5). The design therefore:

- uses neutral labels throughout;
- keeps Ethos & Values (Courage: "taking action despite your fears") as the ethical anchor;
- leaves a single place in Part 1 and in T3 where approved terminology can be added later without restructuring.

## 5. What Section Commanders need for this (feeds T3)

- Be able to demonstrate tactical breathing and one refocus technique.
- Be able to run Prepare → Perform → Recover around the Day 2 task and the exercises.
- Know the six mechanisms well enough to recognise them in recruits' behaviour.
- Don't present the material as Combat Mindset doctrine until the Framework is approved.
