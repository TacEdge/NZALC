# 03 — Development Loop Design

*How EXPERIENCE → FEEDBACK → REFLECT → ADAPT → REAPPLY ↺ works for an Army recruit after Day 2, simple enough to survive a busy TAD programme.*

---

## 1. Design rules

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| L1 | **One priority at a time.** The loop turns on a single behaviour (01, M1) until the recruit and their Section Commander agree to change it |
| L2 | **Conversation before paperwork.** A short conversation that achieves the developmental effect beats a form that records it |
| L3 | **Attach to what already happens.** Use existing AARs, end-of-day section briefs, inspections, the *Rest, Revise, Reflect* routine and exercises. Nothing new is scheduled just for the loop (DR-4) |
| L4 | **Record only what the next review needs.** The workbook holds the evidence thread, not a diary |
| L5 | **The recruit drives; the Section Commander prompts.** The recruit owns it (OC-4, DR-5). The Section Commander asks, observes and occasionally tells |
| L6 | **REAPPLY needs a cue.** Behaviour changes when the recruit remembers the priority *at the moment it matters*. The priority must be carried and seen |

---

## 2. What each step means for a recruit

| Step | Plain-language meaning for the recruit | Recruit's question |
|---|---|---|
| **EXPERIENCE** | Normal TAD training: drill, PT, inspections, lessons, section tasks, field activity, exercises | *Did a moment come up where my priority mattered?* |
| **FEEDBACK** | Evidence from outside my own head: what my buddy saw, what my Section Commander said, what happened as a result | *What did others see? What happened?* |
| **REFLECT** | Making sense of it: comparing what I meant to do with what I did and how it landed | *What does that tell me?* |
| **ADAPT** | Choosing a specific adjustment, or keeping the priority as it is | *Keep it, sharpen it, or change it?* |
| **REAPPLY** | Trying it deliberately in the next relevant moment | *When's my next chance?* |

REAPPLY is the next EXPERIENCE, so the loop closes on itself.

---

## 3. Three intensities of the loop

The loop runs at different intensities, so that most turns cost almost nothing and a few turns are thorough.

| Intensity | When | What happens | Typical cost to the recruit | Typical cost to the Section Commander | Recorded? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Everyday** | Any day the priority came up | Recruit notices; buddy may comment; Section Commander may ask one question | Seconds to a minute | Seconds, and only sometimes | **No** (optional one-line note) |
| **Checkpoint** | Between the two days and Ex One, and between Ex One and Ex Two. **TIMETABLE DEPENDENT** | Buddy check-in ("Did you do it? What did I see?"). Recruit writes one line: what happened, keep or adjust | Minutes | None required; may join if convenient | **One line** |
| **Major** | Ex One and Ex Two | Pressure thread around the exercise; structured self, peer and Section Commander feedback round; review of the previous priority/LDP; new or confirmed LDP | Part of a facilitated session | Facilitates the round; gives one observation per recruit | **Yes**: exercise review + LDP |

**DR-1 (reapply before review)** is met by the checkpoint level: before each major review, the recruit has reapplied the priority and received buddy evidence at least once. The **number** of checkpoints is **TIMETABLE DEPENDENT** and is not prescribed (D17). The minimum is one before Ex One and one between Ex One and Ex Two.

## 4. Step-by-step: who does what (minimum)

### 4.1 Everyday intensity

| Step | Recruit | Buddy | Section Commander (minimum useful intervention) | Workbook |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EXPERIENCE | Trains normally; notices when a moment for the priority comes up | — | — | — |
| FEEDBACK | Notices the result; may ask buddy "how did I go?" | Mentions it if they saw it (optional) | *Occasionally:* "You said your priority was X. How did you go today?" | — |
| REFLECT | Thinks it through, in the answer to the question if one was asked | — | Listens; may add one observation | — |
| ADAPT | Usually keeps going | — | — | — |
| REAPPLY | Tries again next time | — | — | Priority visible wherever Part 2 lives (format unresolved) as a cue |

### 4.2 Checkpoint intensity (TIMETABLE DEPENDENT placement)

| Step | Recruit | Buddy | Section Commander | Workbook |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EXPERIENCE | Recalls the moments since the last check | Recalls what they saw | — | — |
| FEEDBACK | Asks buddy: "Did you see me do it? What happened?" | Gives one honest observation (what, when, effect) | Not required. Optional if a natural moment arises | — |
| REFLECT | "Is it working? What's getting in the way?" | — | — | — |
| ADAPT | Keep / sharpen / change the priority | — | If a recruit wants to *change* priority, a quick word with the Section Commander | — |
| REAPPLY | States when the next chance is | — | — | **One line:** date · what happened · keep/adjust |

**Candidate attachment points** (to confirm with TAD): the *Rest, Revise, Reflect* evening routine (workbook p.49); end-of-week section brief; existing post-activity AARs. **TIMETABLE DEPENDENT.**

### 4.3 Major intensity (Ex One, Ex Two)

| Step | Recruit | Peers | Section Commander | Workbook |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (PREPARE) | Reviews priority; picks a tool; states what they'll try | Know each other's priorities (shared on Day 2) | Brief reminder before the exercise: "Know your priority" | Priority + checkpoint lines |
| EXPERIENCE (PERFORM) | Exercise | Observe their buddy (and others) | Observes; notes one behaviour per recruit | — |
| FEEDBACK (RECOVER) | Self-assessment (DEB/DESC); receives peer and Section Commander feedback in the section round | Give DEB/DESC feedback (strength + development area) | **Facilitates the feedback round**; gives one observation per recruit | Self-assessment; peer feedback summary |
| REFLECT | Compares self-view, peer view and the Section Commander's view; reviews the previous priority: *did I do it, what does the evidence say?* | — | Asks one or two questions if the recruit is stuck | Exercise review page |
| ADAPT | Writes or updates the LDP: priority, evidence, strategy (Keep/Stop/Start for a development area), situations, "how will I know?" | — | Supports, doesn't write it (OC-4) | **LDP** |
| REAPPLY | Carries the new or confirmed priority into training | — | Notes it so they can ask about it | Priority updated in Part 2 |

## 5. Where Keep / Stop / Start lives now

| Kept | Not used |
|---|---|
| In the **LDP** at Ex One, Ex Two and march-out, for development-area strategies | Day 1–2 (replaced by "When ___, I will ___") |
| In the pressure debrief, as Stop / Start (optional) | Everyday and checkpoint intensities |

This cuts the current ~27 Keep/Stop/Start instances to about 3–4 meaningful ones. It is subject to D7 (whether Keep/Stop/Start is an LDS-standard tool, and how often it is required).

---

## 6. The evidence thread (what is kept, end to end)

```
Day 1  identity statement (V1) ─ peer + Section Commander observations (V2, V3) ─ first insight
Day 2  buddy evidence on the reapply (V4) ─ pressure debrief (V5) ─ FIRST PRIORITY
TAD    checkpoint line(s) ─────────────────────────────── TIMETABLE DEPENDENT
Ex One self + peer + Section Commander feedback ─ review of first priority ─ LDP-1
TAD    checkpoint line(s) ─────────────────────────────── TIMETABLE DEPENDENT
Ex Two feedback ─ "did LDP-1 work?" ─ LDP-2
March-out  identity statement revisited: "what evidence shows I've changed?"
           section commitment revisited ─ priority for next unit
```

Any line in the final LDP can be traced back to a specific event and specific feedback (supporting test 6 in the Phase 2 brief).

## 7. Failure modes and design responses

| Failure mode | Likely cause | Design response |
|---|---|---|
| Recruit forgets the priority | No cue at the moment it matters | Priority carried visibly (05); Section Commander's question acts as a cue; buddy knows it |
| Buddy check-ins don't happen | Nobody prompts; buddy pairs are weak | Attach to an existing routine (TIMETABLE DEPENDENT); Section Commander gives a one-line reminder at the section brief |
| Priority is too vague to observe ("be a better team member") | Weak ADAPT step | "When ___, I will ___" format; Section Commander checks on Day 2 (2E) |
| Recruit picks a "safe" priority | Low trust; fear of judgement | Section Commander models it (shares their own development point); evidence is not graded (see 04 §5) |
| Loop stalls after Ex One | Big gap with no prompt | At least one checkpoint between Ex One and Ex Two (DR-1) |
| Section Commander becomes an administrator | Checking logs, signing pages | Explicitly excluded from the role (04) |
| Peer feedback becomes hostile or empty | Low skill; section friction | Single format (DEB/DESC); practised on Day 1; Section Commander facilitates major rounds |
| Recruit has a genuine wellbeing issue surface | Pressure, reflection | Section Commander knows the referral pathway (04); mental-health content is duty of care, not performance |
