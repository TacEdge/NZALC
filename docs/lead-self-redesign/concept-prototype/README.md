# Lead Self — Concept Demonstrator

**What this is.** A clickable HTML prototype of the recruit experience implied by the frozen Phase 2 architecture. Fifteen representative moments from Day 1 of the two Section Commander-delivered Lead Self days through to march-out, told as one developmental story.

**What it is not.** Not Phase 3. Not the new workbook. Not a timetable, a lesson plan, a decision on Part 2's physical format, or a redesign of the T3 package. The architecture it demonstrates is **provisionally accepted, pending user validation** (`../phase-2/10-validation-plan.md`); nothing here changes that.

## Open it

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build, no server. It loads one Google Fonts stylesheet; without a network connection it falls back to Helvetica/Arial and works the same.

## Navigate it

| Control | Does |
|---|---|
| **Next / Back** (bottom bar), or ← → keys | Move one moment at a time |
| Dropdown (bottom bar) | Jump to any moment |
| Journey strip (top) | Shows which of the five questions the current moment serves |
| **Sect Comd** (top right) | Shows what the Section Commander does at this moment |
| **Reviewer** (top right) | Shows design status: settled / provisional / timetable dependent / validation / format or assessment unresolved, with references to the Phase 2 documents |

Example entries are pre-filled (a fictional recruit's two days and Exercise One). Edit anything: later screens pick up what you type (identity statement, priority, tool, evidence). Entries are kept in your browser only; use "Start again" from the last screen or clear site data to reset.

## What to focus on when reviewing

Use `REVIEW-GUIDE.md` (ten questions). The two moments the architecture depends on most are **6 (Feedback)** and **9 (Reapply)**: together they are the loop's first turn. The two moments that are least certain are **12 (Everyday)** and **13 (Checkpoint)**: they depend on Section Commander practice and on what recruits actually carry, which is what the validation conversations test.

Turn on **Reviewer** before forming a view on anything in Part 2. Those screens show function, not format.

## What it deliberately does not decide

- The physical or digital form of Part 2 (09 B1).
- The Day 1 task (the routine-planning task is the leading candidate, 09 O9).
- The Day 2 pressure task.
- Placement of any checkpoint, exercise or march-out activity (all **TIMETABLE DEPENDENT**).
- Which topics get explicit instruction. The six shown are the strongest candidates, not a locked set (06 §1.4).
- Whether any evidence shown is used for formal assessment (09 B6).
- Combat Mindset terminology. Mechanism labels are neutral placeholders; WIN is not used.

`DESIGN-NOTES.md` maps every moment to the architecture, the source material, the Learning Outcomes and its settled/provisional status.
