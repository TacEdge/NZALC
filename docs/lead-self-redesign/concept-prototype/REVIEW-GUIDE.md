# Concept Demonstrator — Review Guide

*Ten questions. Answer from the recruit's seat first; turn on the Section Commander and Reviewer views second. Note the screen number with each comment. The point is to test the experience the architecture produces, not to approve the architecture again or to judge visual polish.*

| # | Question | Look at |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Does this feel like Lead Self?** Is it recognisably the NZDF Leadership Framework, the Value Adds and the Behaviour Statements at work, or has it drifted into generic self-development? | 2, 3, 7, 14 |
| 2 | **Is the recruit doing enough, rather than reading?** Across the fifteen screens, where would a recruit be passive for too long? | all; especially 2, 3, 8 |
| 3 | **Is Identity vs Reputation genuinely experienced?** By the end of screen 6, would a recruit have felt the gap between how they meant to come across and what others saw, or only been told about it? | 4, 5, 6 |
| 4 | **Is the one-priority mechanism clear?** Is it obvious why one, how it's written, who knows it, and what happens to it next? | 7, 9, 12, 13 |
| 5 | **Does the loop make intuitive sense after experiencing it?** When screen 10 names it, does "you've already done this" ring true from screens 5–9? | 10 |
| 6 | **Does Day 2 feel overloaded?** Zones, interference, tools, a pressure task, a debrief, a priority, a section commitment and a handover. What would you cut or move? | 8, 9, 10, 11 |
| 7 | **Is the transition into ongoing TAD development believable?** Would this actually keep going after Day 2 in a real section, with a real Corporal? What would stop it? | 11, 12, 13 |
| 8 | **Is anything still unnecessarily academic?** Point to any word, model or sentence an 18-year-old recruit would skip. | all |
| 9 | **Is anything important from the existing package being lost?** Check the full-text reference on screen 3 and the Reviewer notes. Name any topic, tool or activity you'd want back as instruction rather than as a lens or reference. | 3, 8, Reviewer view |
| 10 | **Could a Corporal realistically deliver this?** Turn on the Section Commander view and read it as a Section Commander with a full week-1 programme. Which moments would you skip, shorten, or need the T3 package to prepare you for? | Section Commander view, all |

**One more, for the project record:** which three screens should be shown to recruits and Section Commanders during validation, if any? (Default position: none, until the conversations in `../phase-2/10-validation-plan.md` are done, so the architecture is tested against current reality first.)
